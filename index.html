01 · Context

Indians already talk to their phones. Just not to ChatGPT.

Voice notes, Google Assistant and Alexa have made “talk instead of type” normal for hundreds of millions of people. Users don’t need to be taught voice. They need a reason to use it inside ChatGPT.

02 · Teardown · What happens when you tap the mic

The misheard question goes straight to the model

Two steps, and no chance to fix anything in between.

Where it breaks for Indian students

No live feedback

Long or accented questions get abandoned mid-sentence.

No review before send

A transcription error is passed on as intent. The model answers the wrong question, confidently.

Weak on Hinglish, names, numbers

The students voice helps most are the ones it transcribes worst.

Others already solved it

Claude shows a live transcript you can edit before sending. WhatsApp lets you pause and cancel. YouTube clearly shows it’s listening. ChatGPT does none of these.

03 · What students told us

Not a discovery problem. A correction-cost problem.

I surveyed 10 college students who study with ChatGPT and interviewed 2. An early signal, but a consistent one.

All 10 say voice suits long questions. All 10 typed their last one.

04 · The problem worth solving

HOW MIGHT WE

…let students fix what voice got wrong, before it reaches the model, so speaking a doubt is faster than typing it?

05 · The solution

A live, editable transcript

Words appear as the student speaks. Stop opens a draft instead of sending. Likely-misheard words are underlined and fixed in one tap. Send is the only way a question leaves the draft.

Today

Tap mic→Speak (blank screen)→Auto-send→Wrong answer→Retype

Proposed

Tap mic→Live words→Review draft→One-tap fix→Send→Right answer
North star

+20% weekly share of prompts sent by voice, among Indian college students

Quality check

−50% wrong questions sent by voice

How we’ll know

4-week 50/50 A/B test with Indian mobile users

Phone screens are simplified recreations; the annotated wireframes are in Figma. Metrics, experiment, rollout and risks are in the PRD.

The takeaway

Students don’t need to be taught voice. They need to fix it before it’s sent.
